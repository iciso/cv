Reverse Order — الإِضَافَة

English puts the owner first ("the Messenger's house"). Arabic flips it: the possession comes first, then the owner ("house of the Messenger"). Tap a word, then tap a slot to place it.

📖 The Word Order Rule
Arabic reads right to left. In the Idafah, the first word on the right is the possession (the thing being possessed). The second word on the left is the owner.
English Owner's possession → "The Messenger's House"
Arabic بَيْتُ الرَّسُولِ

Read the Arabic right to left:

بَيْتُ = "house" — the possession, and the first word.
الرَّسُولِ = "of the Messenger" — the owner, and the second word.

So the Arabic phrase literally reads: "House of the Messenger". Your task in every puzzle below is to place the possession first (on the right) and the owner second (on the left).
