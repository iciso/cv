Arabic Pronouns — الضَّمَائِر

Master all 12 Arabic pronouns — both the detached (standalone) and the attached (suffix) forms — across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd person, singular, dual, and plural, masculine and feminine. All examples drawn from the Noble Qur'an.

📖 Grammar Reference — the full pronoun table

Arabic has twelve pronouns, each with two forms:

• Detached (منفصل) — a standalone word that can begin a sentence: أَنَا (I), هُوَ (he).
• Attached (متصل) — a suffix that never stands alone, always joined to a noun, verb or particle: ـِي (my), ـهُ (his).
