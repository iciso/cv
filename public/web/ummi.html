بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم

Mother

Have Mercy on my Sweet Tender Mother, as well as those who lost theirs, and all mothers who exhausted a good part of her Life in nurturing us, spent sleepless nights when we fell sick, sacrificed her sleep and rest for us, readily gave us her share of any delicacy when we asked her, toiled physically day and night, doing all the house hold chores, growing vegetables, looking after poultry and cattle, and the shopping for all the needs of the household. Or whatever tasks and work that Mothers are engaged in all their lives.

Grant Success to all Mothers in their worldly lives and in their Hereafter. Relieve all mothers from the pain and suffering they are going through. Ya Rabb, Mothers in their old age are as tender, soft and sweet as babies and cannot take pain. Ya Rabb, if you like any of my good deeds, which I had done in any part of my life, I beg you to accept it and make it as the Waseela and cure all the righteous Muslim mothers completely and heal them and remove from them the pain and suffering soon.

Ya Rahman, you are Seventy times more Merciful to your servants than our Mothers. Shower your Rays of Mercy on all of them, Cleanse all their Sins and Make all mothers Pure and White as Snow and Grant all mothers a Husunul Khatimah at the time You have Ordained for them and Admit all the mothers to Jannathul Firdaus Al Aala. Aameen Aameen Ya Rabb Al Aalameen.

Mothers are by their very nature unobtrusive, as their absence is most felt, and their presence is mostly unseen, and unsung.

Rafique
