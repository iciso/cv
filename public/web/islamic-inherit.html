Reference Note on Sunni Ilm al-Fara'id Methodology:

Calculations follow classical Sunni jurisprudence based on Surah An-Nisa (verses 11, 12, and 176) and the prophetic directive: "Give the fixed shares (Fara'id) to those entitled to them, and whatever remains goes to the nearest male agnate (Asabah)." (Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim). For complex estates involving Dhawu al-Arham (distant uterine relatives when no Furud or Asabah exist), hermaphrodites (Khuntha), missing persons (Mafqud), or unborn fetuses (Haml), consult a qualified Mufti or Islamic judicial council.
