3. What is happening inside the body?

The simulated day is ready. Press Run the day, then add a meal or activity.
🍚 Gut and food
Carbohydrates are digested and absorbed. Their appearance in the bloodstream takes time.
🟠 Pancreas and insulin
Insulin helps regulate glucose, including by promoting uptake in muscle and fat and suppressing liver glucose output.
🟢 Muscle
Muscle is a major site of glucose disposal. Activity can increase muscle glucose uptake.
🟣 Liver
The liver stores and releases glucose. It contributes to glucose regulation between meals and overnight.

6. Safety and interpretation

Educational simulation only — not for clinical decisions.

This game is not a glucose monitor, diagnostic instrument, treatment planner or insulin calculator. Never use it to choose, change or calculate insulin or other medication doses.

The simulated values, food indices, portion effects and response times are invented teaching parameters, not validated physiological predictions. They are not measured nutritional values or individual glucose forecasts.

Real glucose responses vary with the individual, diabetes type, medication, food preparation, portion, illness, sleep, hormones and many other factors. Clinical decisions require individual assessment and appropriate professional guidance. Hypoglycaemia can be dangerous and requires appropriate recognition and management.
